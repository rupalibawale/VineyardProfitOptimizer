# VineyardProfitOptimizer

This project presents a comprehensive analysis of grape farming profitability among marginal farmers in rural Maharashtra, specifically in Nashik and Sangli districts. Through a combination of real-world survey data and simulated datasets, the study investigates the economic viability of grape cultivation and the potential role of Farmer Producer Companies (FPCs) in improving returns. Using Python-based data analysis tools, the study explores profitability drivers such as landholding size, yield per acre, cost per acre, percentage of produce exported, and price realization. Scenario modeling for GAP adoption and enhanced export access is also undertaken.

Key findings indicate a strong link between profitability and export market access, suggesting the critical role of policy support and collective farmer action. The report concludes with targeted recommendations for government, cooperatives, and farmer networks.
